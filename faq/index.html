Questions before you choose.

This page displays common questions from architects, interior designers, contractors, retailers and furniture makers planning material purchases in Ahmedabad.

Which plywood is suitable for furniture and wardrobes?

The right grade depends on the room, moisture exposure, design and budget. Share the application, thickness and quantity with us for a practical recommendation.

Do you supply architects, interior designers and contractors?

Yes. We supply plywood, MDF, blockboard, flushdoors, laminates and timber for professional projects, retail requirements and furniture workshops across Ahmedabad and nearby areas.

What information should I include in a material inquiry?

Include the application, material or grade, thickness, sheet size, approximate quantity, finish preference and delivery location so the team can respond accurately.

Do you supply MR, commercial and packing grade plywood?

Yes. The plywood range includes alternate, MR grade, commercial and packing grade options. Product availability can vary, so request a current recommendation for your project.
