About The House

Since 2001, built on trust that feels quiet but lasting.

Shreeji Sales Corporation has grown as a plywood and wood-product wholesaler by staying useful: clear product guidance, dependable supply conversations, and a grounded understanding of how projects actually get executed.

What We Do

We help buyers move from “which one?” to “this is the right material.”

Our range includes commercial plywood, MR grade plywood, alternate plywood, packing grade plywood, blockboard, flushdoor, MDF, laminate, and timber. The value is not only in offering them, but in helping customers understand when each one makes sense.
